## 🎯 Project Overview

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data, but raw numbers alone
rarely drive decisions. This project transforms Superstore sales data into a clear,
interactive dashboard that highlights performance across regions, product categories,
and time — and adds a short-term sales forecast to support planning.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Analysis Areas

- **Sales Performance** — Total sales, profit, order quantity, and average delivery time
- **Customer Behavior** — Sales split by payment mode and customer segment
- **Product Performance** — Sales by category, sub-category, and top-selling products
- **Regional Trends** — Sales and profit distribution across U.S. states
- **Forecasting** — 15-day sales forecast based on historical order trends

---

## 📌 Dashboard Features

- **Two-Page Layout** — a Sales Overview page and a dedicated Forecast page
- **Interactive Region Filter** — slice all visuals by Central, East, South, or West
- **KPI Summary Cards** — instant visibility into core sales and profit metrics
- **Year-over-Year Trends** — monthly sales and profit compared across 2019 and 2020
- **Time Series Forecasting** — Power BI's built-in forecasting applied to the sales trend
